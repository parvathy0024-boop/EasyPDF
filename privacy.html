Welcome to EasyPDF. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit our website.

EasyPDF does not currently require visitors to create an account or provide personal information to use its available PDF tools.

Our hosting provider and other technical services may process information such as IP addresses, browser details, device information and server logs when you visit the website.

2. PDF Files and Document Processing

The currently available PDF tools process selected documents in your browser using JavaScript. The website is not designed to upload these selected documents to an EasyPDF file-storage server.

The PDF processing library is loaded from a third-party content delivery network. Your use of the website may also involve third-party network requests.

Please avoid using public or shared computers for sensitive documents. Always keep your original files and check your downloaded documents.
